My strongest recommendation
Adaptive Multi-Elevator Dispatch System Using Policy Iteration
Core idea
Instead of asking:
“Which floor should the elevator go to next?”
make the problem:
“Given the current traffic conditions in a building, which elevator should serve which passenger requests, in what order, while minimizing waiting time, travel time, energy consumption and unnecessary elevator movement?”
That turns it into a genuine sequential decision-making problem.
Imagine a 20-floor building with 3 elevators.
At 8:50 AM:
Floor 1
 ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓
People want to go to:
10, 12, 15, 18, 20
At 1:00 PM:
Requests distributed randomly
throughout the building
At 5:30 PM:
20,18,15,12,10
 ↓  ↓  ↓  ↓  ↓
Most people want to reach Floor 1
A fixed elevator algorithm will behave the same way in all three situations.
Your RL system should not.
It should learn that different traffic conditions require different policies.
